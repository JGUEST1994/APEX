# Market intelligence: reference files

These files back the `apex-market-intelligence` skill (news and events feeds, the
opportunity scanner, and company fundamentals). They are also plain guides for anyone
adding those layers by hand.

| File | Use it when |
|---|---|
| [sources.md](sources.md) | Choosing a feed: the approved free sources, their limits, and what to avoid |
| [feed-checklist.md](feed-checklist.md) | Adding a new feed, and checking it is point in time |
| [evidence-report.md](evidence-report.md) | Reporting whether a news filter or scanner idea helped |

News, scanner hits and fundamentals are inputs to existing strategies, filters and the
meta-labelling model. They never place trades on their own, and APEX stays research and
paper only.
